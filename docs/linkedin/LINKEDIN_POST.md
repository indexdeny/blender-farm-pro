Blender Farm Pro explores a practical question: how can packaging visualization become more controllable and inspectable?

I defined the requirements, designed the architecture and technical specifications, coordinated AI agents, and evaluated the resulting renders. The pipeline connects structured SKU data and artwork inputs to validated manifests, editable Blender scenes and Cycles renders.

Two examples — Viagra and Cialis — reached owner-reported workplace acceptance. In the checked Blender 5.1.2 CPU environment, the preserved scenes reproduced the accepted pixels exactly; PNG hashes also matched after the documented metadata policy. Fresh procedural generation was checked separately against its prior baseline.

The carousel shows development stages, not an isolated automation effect: geometry, camera, artwork and visual design were refined together. View differences and unknown capture dates limit chronological comparison.

This remained an R&D prototype. Further catalogue development stopped because of budget constraints. These independent portfolio examples are not manufacturer-certified packaging; publication permission is owner-confirmed within the documented portfolio scope.
